All products & restock guide

# Product Units sold Trend Share Orders Avg / day (all sales) In stock Days of cover Runs out about Suggested order Status

Days of cover = current stock ÷ average units sold per day in the selected range. Suggested order brings stock up to your target days of cover. Trend compares units sold with the period just before the selected range. Runs out about assumes sales continue at the current average. Use a range of 30 days or more for stable numbers — a single busy day can make short ranges misleading. When a "Sales from" filter is on, units, share and orders show only that campaign's sales, but days of cover and suggested orders still use all sales, because restocking has to cover every customer. Stock figures come from the Quantity column of your Stock tab, so products without a quantity there show "No stock data".
